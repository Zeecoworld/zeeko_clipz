Up and running in three steps

No video editor, no manual uploads.

Connect your accounts

Sign in to YouTube and TikTok from each platform's Settings tab. Each one has its own login.

Upload a CSV per platform

One row per video, in the order you want them used. Add chapters or a transcript link if you want cuts at natural breaks.

Let the queue run

Clips are cut, scheduled and posted automatically. Check the dashboard to see progress and retry anything that failed.

One tool, two platforms, two playbooks

Each platform is tuned separately.

YouTube Shorts

5 posts a day
  • Uploads through the official YouTube Data API
  • Titles with "Part 2/5" numbering and an automatic #Shorts tag
  • Retries on its own if the daily API quota is reached

TikTok

10 posts a day
  • Uploads through the official TikTok Content Posting API
  • Inbox mode delivers each clip for you to publish, or direct posting once TikTok approves the app
  • Waits and retries when TikTok's pending-upload limit is reached

Questions people ask

Straight answers, including the limits.

Will my posts be public right away?

That depends on each platform's review of this app. Until YouTube completes its API audit, uploads made through the API are held as private. Until TikTok approves direct posting, clips are delivered to your TikTok inbox or posted as private. Once the platforms approve the app, posts follow the privacy setting you choose.

Does it work with YouTube page links or Google Drive links?

Video lengths are detected from direct MP4 or MOV file links. For other link types, add the duration in the CSV yourself.

Can I post the same clip on both platforms?

Yes. The two queues are independent, so the same video can appear in both CSVs. There is also an option to keep identical clips at least 24 hours apart.

What happens if a post fails?

It is marked as failed with the reason, and you can retry it from the dashboard. Quota and rate-limit errors are retried automatically.

What do you store about me?

Only what is needed to post for you: the connection tokens for your accounts and your queue of clips. See the Privacy Policy for details, including how to disconnect and delete your data.
