Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Clip Studio (the "Service"), operated by Zeecomedia Tech Enterprises, Lagos, Nigeria ("we", "us"), collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and other laws that apply to us.

In short: we store only what is needed to post for you. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and you can disconnect your accounts and ask us to delete your data at any time.

1. Information we collect

  • Connection details for your accounts. When you connect YouTube or TikTok, the platform gives us access and refresh tokens, the permissions (scopes) you granted, and, for TikTok, your display name. These let us upload on your behalf.
  • Content you submit. Links to your video files, headlines, titles, captions, chapter times and transcript links from the CSV files you upload.
  • Queue records. The clips created from your videos, their scheduled times, their status (scheduled, publishing, posted, failed), any error message, and the ID the platform gives each upload.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider records standard request logs, such as IP address, browser type and timestamps, for security and troubleshooting.
  • Sign-in session. A session token is kept in your browser so you stay logged in. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

We do not collect your platform passwords. You sign in on Google's and TikTok's own pages.

2. How we use information

  • To cut your videos into clips and upload the clips you scheduled to the accounts you connected.
  • To show you your queue, statistics and any errors.
  • To retry failed uploads and keep the Service secure and working.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or to train machine learning models.

3. Data from Google and YouTube

The Service requests the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) only. We use it solely to upload videos that you scheduled to your own channel. We do not read your channel data, your other videos, your subscribers or your analytics.

The Service's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The Service also uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data from TikTok

The Service requests permission to read your basic profile (your display name, to show which account is connected) and to upload videos to your account. We use these permissions only to deliver or post videos you scheduled. We do not collect your followers, messages or other account content.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with the service providers needed to run the Service, and with the platforms you connect:

  • Netlify hosts the website and runs our server functions.
  • Supabase stores the database holding connection tokens and queue records.
  • Cloudinary receives the link to your source video and produces the clips. Processed clips are served from Cloudinary's network.
  • YouTube (Google) and TikTok receive the videos, titles and captions you scheduled, as required to publish them.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the rights and safety of others.

6. How long we keep it

  • Connection tokens are kept until you disconnect the platform. Disconnecting from the Settings tab deletes them straight away.
  • Queue records are kept until you ask us to delete them or we stop operating the Service.
  • Hosting logs are kept for the period set by our hosting provider.
  • Cloudinary copies of processed clips remain in Cloudinary's cache until they expire under its policies.

7. Revoking access and deleting your data

  • Disconnect a platform at any time in that platform's Settings tab.
  • Revoke YouTube access in your Google Account permissions.
  • Remove TikTok access in the TikTok app, under your account's security and permissions settings.
  • To have your queue records deleted, email us at REPLACE_ME_EMAIL. We will act on your request within a reasonable time and in line with the law.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, withdraw consent, and complain to a data protection authority. In Nigeria that authority is the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Account tokens are stored in a database that can only be reached through our server functions, not from your browser. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect the information we hold.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 18.

11. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where that happens we rely on those providers' safeguards for protecting personal data.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant we will make it visible in the Service.
